Privacy policy

A neighbourhood book-lending library run by volunteers. Last updated 23 October 2026.

What we store

  • Your WhatsApp number, so the library's WhatsApp number can message you.
  • The display name, building and locality you choose.
  • The books you add, your borrow requests, loans and the messages you exchange with other members about a request.
  • Books you asked to be notified about, and whether you want messages about new books.
  • Who invited you (if you joined with someone's invite link), so admins know who vouched for you.
  • For security, the server keeps a log of visits (internet address, page and time) for 7 days, to spot and block attacks. It doesn't record what you search for, sign-in links or invite codes.

Who can see what

  • Other members see your display name, building/locality and the books you've listed.
  • Your phone number is never shown to anyone, unless you tap “Share my number” on a specific request; then only that one person sees it.
  • Messages about a request are visible only to the two people in that request.
  • Every new member is approved by an admin, who sees the newcomer's name, building/locality and who invited them (never their number). Until approved, a newcomer can see which books exist but not who owns them.
  • The library's admins see member names and usage counts in the app. The volunteer who runs the server could technically open its database, but does not read members' messages or numbers.

Services involved

  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries the messages between you and the library's number, under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
  • When you add a book, its ISBN or title is looked up on Open Library and Google Books to fill in details. No personal information is sent.
  • If shelf-photo recognition is turned on, photos you send to add books are processed by Anthropic to read the titles and are not stored by the library.
  • The server uses CrowdSec to block attackers. It shares the internet addresses that attack the server (and how) with CrowdSec's network, and nothing else.

We don't sell or share your information with anyone else, show ads, or send promotional messages.

Your choices

  • Change your name, building or locality any time (Me tab, or name …, building …, locality … on WhatsApp).
  • Remove any book you've listed.
  • Delete your account: send delete me to the library's WhatsApp number, or use “Delete my account” on the Me tab. Your account, books, requests and messages are removed straight away. Daily backups that may contain them are erased after 14 days.

Contact

Questions or concerns? Message the library's WhatsApp number, or ask the volunteer who invited you.
